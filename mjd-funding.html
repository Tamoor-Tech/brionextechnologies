MJD Funding

A professional digital experience for a funding business, designed to present financial services and help visitors explore funding solutions.

Project Overview

MJD Funding is a commercial finance enterprise providing working capital, revenue-based financing, and term funding for expanding businesses. They needed a contemporary digital presence to convey institutional legitimacy, guide prospective business owners through capital alternatives, and encourage qualified funding inquiries.

Business Requirements & Objectives

  • Instill Financial Credibility: High-trust visual language to differentiate MJD Funding from opaque lending brokers.
  • Program Transparency: Clear presentation of financing terms, approval timelines (24-48 hours), and program limits ($25,000 to $5,000,000+).
  • Interactive Estimation: An intuitive client-side capital estimator to help business owners conceptualize funding parameters prior to applying.
  • Mobile Friction Reduction: Seamless application entry points optimized for commercial decision-makers accessing the site via smartphones.

Design Approach

We established a tailored deep navy and carbon color foundation (`#091428`), paired with crisp typography and high-trust cyan and emerald accents. Rather than generic stock photos of handshakes, the UI relies on structured metric badges, transparent data tables, and clean interactive controls that mirror modern financial tech dashboards.

Development Approach

The frontend was engineered with lightweight, semantic HTML5, modular CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript. We eliminated external heavy dependencies, ensuring that page weight remained strictly under 300KB. This sub-second rendering guarantees optimal Core Web Vitals scores and instantaneous responsiveness on cellular connections.
