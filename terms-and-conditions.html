Terms and Conditions

Last Updated: November 2026

Notice: This document serves as a starter commercial terms template outlining standard software and web development practices. It does not constitute legal counsel and should be reviewed and customized legally to meet your business's jurisdiction.

1. Scope of Digital Services

Brionex Technologies provides digital agency services including custom website development, mobile application engineering, custom software solutions, website redesigns, search engine optimization, branding, and social media marketing. Detailed project scopes, deliverable schedules, and specifications are formalized in individual project proposals or statements of work (SOW).

2. Intellectual Property Ownership

Upon receipt of full payment for all agreed project milestones, 100% of the custom source code, design assets, logos, and digital deliverables created specifically for the client become the sole intellectual property of the client. Brionex Technologies retains the right to reference non-confidential project visuals and live links for portfolio and agency demonstration purposes unless an explicit Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) specifies otherwise.

3. Milestone Payments & Delivery

Projects are typically executed on milestone payment schedules (e.g., initial deposit upon scoping approval, intermediate prototype sign-off, and final settlement upon live deployment). Invoices must be settled in accordance with the timeline terms stated in the approved project proposal.

4. Client Responsibilities & Materials

The client is responsible for supplying required content, logos, third-party API credentials, domain access, and operational guidelines necessary for timely delivery. Delays in providing necessary client feedback or materials may proportionately adjust agreed project delivery milestones.

5. Revisions & Scope Adjustments

Each project phase incorporates iterative review cycles. Any feature requests or functional alterations outside the agreed initial specification will be documented and estimated as an addendum to ensure complete transparency before implementation.

6. Warranties & Support

Brionex Technologies warrants that deliverables are free of intentional code defects and comply with modern web standards at the time of delivery. A standard post-launch support period is provided to resolve any latent bugs in the agreed scope. Continued maintenance, external server outages, or third-party API deprecations can be supported under separate retainer agreements.
