Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026

Notice: This document serves as a professional starter privacy policy template provided for baseline informational structuring. It does not constitute formal legal counsel and should be reviewed by qualified legal counsel to meet your jurisdiction's specific statutory requirements.

1. Information We Collect

When you visit the Brionex Technologies website or submit an inquiry through our contact mechanisms, we may collect information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Your full name and contact email address.
  • Company or business name and designated contact title.
  • Project requirements, specifications, and estimated budget ranges.
  • Standard technical metadata collected automatically by web hosting servers (IP address, browser type, referring URLs, and timestamps).

2. How We Use Collected Information

Information collected is strictly utilized to facilitate commercial collaboration, including:

  • Reviewing your project brief and preparing custom technical proposals.
  • Direct communication via email or WhatsApp regarding your requested services.
  • Improving website stability, security, and performance.

3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

Brionex Technologies does not sell, rent, or lease client personal information to third parties. Data is only shared with trusted infrastructure providers (such as hosting servers and email dispatch gateways) necessary to deliver requested communications and services.

4. Security Measures

We enforce standard digital security protocols, including HTTPS encryption across all pages and secure form routing, to protect your submitted project information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our static website utilizes standard functional cookies and temporary session identifiers to ensure smooth page navigation. We do not deploy invasive third-party tracking networks or sell advertising identifiers.
