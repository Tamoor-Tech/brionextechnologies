Pricing Clarifications

Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Answers to common commercial questions before requesting a proposal.

Every business has unique scope requirements: page count, custom features, third-party integrations, and design complexity. Agencies advertising one-size-fits-all fixed prices typically inflate baseline fees or cut corners with generic templates. We calculate tailored estimates so you only invest in what your project actually requires.

Typically, projects are divided into structured milestones (e.g. 40% initial deposit upon specification sign-off, 30% upon interactive prototype approval, and 30% upon final quality testing and deployment). This ensures aligned incentives and predictable delivery.

There are no mandatory ongoing fees paid to Brionex. You only cover standard third-party infrastructure (e.g. your domain registrar, hosting service like Vercel or DigitalOcean). If you desire ongoing maintenance, software updates, or content additions, we offer optional monthly support retainers.
